## 🌟 About Us
**Mewar University** is an autonomous, self-financed private state university promoted by the Mewar Education Society. Established by the Government of Rajasthan and accredited by **NAAC**, the university is a premier destination for multi-disciplinary higher education, technical innovation, and value-based learning.

Our GitHub organization serves as a central hub for student projects, academic research repositories, open-source campus collaborations, and tech club initiatives.

## 🚀 Guidelines for Student Contributors
Are you a student or faculty member at Mewar University? Here is how you can get involved:
1. **Request Access:** Contact your department's lab administrator or campus tech lead to be added as a member of this GitHub organization.
2. **Repository Naming:** Keep repository names clear and relevant to your project or subject code (e.g., `btech-cse-final-project`).
3. **Open Source:** We highly encourage contributing to open-source initiatives and collaborating across different batches and branches.
4. **Code of Conduct:** Ensure all code uploaded is original, well-documented, and adheres to institutional academic integrity policies.
